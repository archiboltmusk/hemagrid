Root Cause Diagnosis

The 4 Breakdowns in India's Blood Supply

Why traditional donation campaigns fail to eliminate emergency panic.

Bottleneck 01

Coerced "Replacement Donation"

The Supreme Court banned paid blood in 1996, yet hospitals routinely make admission or surgery conditional upon the patient's distraught relatives arranging replacement donors. This generates black markets, emergency trauma, and compromised transfusion safety.

HemaGrid Fix: 100% voluntary 365-day rolling donor pipelines and auto-clearing between facilities.
Bottleneck 02

The 5-Day Platelet Expiry Paradox

Platelet concentrates have a strict 5-day shelf life at 20°C–24°C with agitation. Standalone blood banks hoard units without real-time inter-bank visibility. Thousands of platelet units expire while cancer and dengue wards nearby desperately search for matching units.

HemaGrid Fix: Expiry Radar algorithm flags units $< 48\text{h}$ and dispatches automatic transfer manifests.
Bottleneck 03

Whole Blood Fractionation Lag

Over 60% of rural and tier-2 blood banks lack Component Separation Units (BCSU). Patients are transfused whole blood when they only need platelets or plasma. This wastes 66% of every collection and increases fluid overload risks in cardiac and pediatric patients.

HemaGrid Fix: Hub-and-Spoke model with vein-to-vein component barcoding and inventory optimization.
Bottleneck 04

Last-Mile Cold-Chain & Maternal Mortality

Postpartum Hemorrhage (PPH) during childbirth is the leading cause of maternal death in rural India. Primary Health Centers (PHCs) cannot store red cells (2°C–6°C) for long periods, and emergency transport takes hours over congested or hilly terrain.

HemaGrid Fix: IoT sensor-monitored smart cold boxes + rapid EV and certified drone corridor dispatch.

Medical & Legal Framework

Built for Indian Healthcare Regulations

Fully compliant with national statutory guidelines and clinical safety protocols.

Drugs & Cosmetics Act (1940)

Complies with Part XII-B and Schedule F rules governing blood bank licensing, processing protocols, cold storage standards, and the strict statutory prohibition of commercial or paid blood donation.

NBTC Screening Protocols

Enforces mandatory testing for Transfusion-Transmissible Infections (HIV 1/2, HBV, HCV, Syphilis, Malaria) and Nucleic Acid Testing (NAT) integration to eliminate window-period infection risks.

DPDP Act (2023) Privacy

Ensures cryptographic privacy for donor health profiles. Prohibits publicly scrapable phone number registries, replacing them with private, encrypted WhatsApp micro-dispatching.

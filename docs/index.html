COMPLETE WORKSPACE MONOREPO

Four Clean Applications Ready in Code

Run each one locally with a single npm command.

PORT 4001

Hospital Screen (ERP & Expiry Radar)

For nurses and blood bank staff. Scans barcodes on blood bags, keeps a live count, and rings an alert when blood is 48 hours away from expiring so it can be moved to a hospital that needs it today.

apps/hospital-app npm run dev:hospital
PORT 4002

Donor & Patient Screen (HemaPass)

For regular citizens. Gives donors a digital card with QR check-in, tracking how many lives they saved. If a family has an emergency, they press one SOS button and the system privately alerts nearby verified donors.

apps/consumer-app npm run dev:consumer
PORT 4003

Logistics & Cold-Chain Console

For delivery couriers and medical drones. Shows live temperatures inside the transport box every 10 seconds. If the box gets warmer than 6°C, it immediately flags the blood so patients never receive spoiled bags.

apps/partner-app npm run dev:partner
PORT 4000

Central Brain & Matching Engine

The central API server that coordinates the entire grid. Checks blood group compatibility, verifies that 5 infection tests are clean, and syncs directly with the central government portal automatically.

apps/api-server npm run dev:api

The 17 Loopholes & How We Fixed Each One

Every loophole in India's blood banking system stopped by code.

LOOPHOLE 01

Paid Blood Brokers Posing as "Family Friends"

Touts take cash from families and pretend to be a cousin to bypass the paid donation ban.

Fixed: ID deduplication flags paid repeat donors.
LOOPHOLE 02

Frequent Unsafe Donations (Draining Donors)

People donating every 3 weeks, draining their body's iron stores.

Fixed: Strict 90-day cooldown software lock.
LOOPHOLE 03

Anemic Women Turned Away and Lost Forever

50%+ of female donors rejected for hemoglobin < 12.5 and never return.

Fixed: Automatic 30-day nutrition coaching in app.
LOOPHOLE 04

Holiday Gluts Followed by Months of Drought

Collecting 5,000 bags on Aug 15 that expire together 35 days later.

Fixed: 365-day rolling corporate & university cycles.
LOOPHOLE 05

Window-Period Infections Slipping Through Simple Tests

Old antibody tests miss early HIV and Hepatitis in the first 2-3 weeks.

Fixed: Individual testing (NAT) cuts risk to 4 days.
LOOPHOLE 06

Issuing Blood Before Lab Tests Finish

Busy technicians printing "OK" stickers before HIV/Hepatitis tests clear.

Fixed: Bags locked in QUARANTINE until signed clean.
LOOPHOLE 07

Wrong Blood Type Given at Bedside

Clerical mix-ups swapping bags between patients in crowded emergency wards.

Fixed: System sounds an alarm and blocks incompatible checkout.
LOOPHOLE 08

Rare Bombay Blood Mistakenly Labeled as O-

Giving normal O- blood to a Bombay blood patient causes fatal kidney damage.

Fixed: Isolated Bombay protocol only allows Bombay blood.
LOOPHOLE 09

Invisible Blood Spoiling on 42°C Indian Roads

Ice melts in traffic; blood gets warm and toxic while still looking red.

Fixed: Smart sensor logs every 10s; auto-locks if > 6°C.
LOOPHOLE 10

Platelet Shaker Stops (Clotted Platelets)

Platelets left stationary on a table clot within 24 hours.

Fixed: Motion sensors verify continuous gentle movement.
LOOPHOLE 11

Village Mothers Dying from Postpartum Bleeding

Rural clinics have no blood; cars take 3 hours over broken roads.

Fixed: Certified medical drone deliveries in < 20 minutes.
LOOPHOLE 12

Hospital Hoarding Leading to Expiry

Private hospitals holding rare units until they expire and get thrown away.

Fixed: Expiry Radar transfers units with < 48 hours left.
LOOPHOLE 13

Forced Family Replacement Donors

Doctors refusing to admit patients until family members arrange replacement donors.

Fixed: System flags any hospital with > 5% replacement rate.
LOOPHOLE 14

Over-Billing & Hidden Testing Charges

Hospitals charging ₹4,000 to ₹8,000 for a bag legally capped at ₹1,450.

Fixed: Invoice validator blocks bills exceeding statutory caps.
LOOPHOLE 15

Pouring Plasma Down the Drain

India spends ₹3,200 Cr importing Albumin while domestic plasma is wasted.

Fixed: Plasma pooling manifests route plasma to domestic medicine plants.
LOOPHOLE 16

Donor Phone Numbers Leaked to Touts

Old apps posted donor phone numbers online, causing harassment.

Fixed: Phone numbers are masked; alerts sent via private WhatsApp.
LOOPHOLE 17

Empty Shelves After Rare Blood Is Taken

Patient takes the last rare unit 20 km away; shelf stays empty until the next crisis.

Fixed: Automatic replenishment texts 2 verified nearby donors.

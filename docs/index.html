STEP-BY-STEP CALM GUIDE

Need Blood in the Next 3 Hours? Follow These 4 Steps

Do not panic. Fresh blood takes 2 hours to test, so finding blood already sitting in a refrigerator is the fastest way.

01

Get Patient Sample Vials

Ask the doctor or ward nurse for 2 labeled blood sample vials of the patient and the signed Blood Requisition Form. You cannot get blood without these.

02

Call the Bank to Reserve

Use the directory above to call the closest blood bank. Say: "I have patient samples and doctor's form. Please hold 1 unit for me while I am traveling."

03

Take an Ice Box

Always carry a small thermocol ice box with ice packs. Never carry blood in a plastic carry-bag; heat destroys blood cells, and the doctor will reject it.

04

Know the Legal Price Cap

The government legally caps blood processing at ₹1,450 maximum for red cells (PRBC). Do not pay touts or middlemen thousands of rupees.

DIAGNOSTIC OF THE CRISIS

The 6 Realities of India's Blood Shortage

National collection totals look sufficient on paper, but ground realities are fractured by poor distribution, systemic inefficiencies, and deep logistical bottlenecks. Here is how HemaGrid addresses each one.

REALITY 01 Solution: HemaShield Family Protection + 365-Day Pools

The Burden of the "Replacement Donor" System

In most hospitals, patients cannot simply draw blood from the hospital's reserve; distressed family members are told to arrange replacement donors before blood is released. Many relatives rush in only to be disqualified by high BP, diabetes, or age, creating panic and forcing families to turn to illegal paid touts.

✓ HemaGrid Fix: Donating once shields your entire family for 12 months with zero replacement demands.
REALITY 02 Solution: Hub-and-Spoke Mesh + Drone Corridors

"Blood Deserts" and Geographic Inequality

Over 70% of rural and semi-urban populations live more than 30 to 60 minutes away from licensed blood centers. Peripheral Community Health Centres (CHCs) lack licensed blood storage, turning the critical "golden hour" in trauma and childbirth hemorrhages into fatal travel delays.

✓ HemaGrid Fix: Mother Hubs supply rural Spokes using smart temperature-monitored boxes and medical drones in < 20 minutes.
REALITY 03 Solution: Scanner-Level Auto-Sync + Inter-Bank Clearinghouse

Lack of Real-Time, Unified Inventory

Data entry across public, charitable, and private blood banks on portals is frequently manual and delayed. A blood bank showing "3 units" online is often empty on the shelf. Regulatory barriers and lack of live software prevent surplus blood in one hospital from moving to another facility 5 km away.

✓ HemaGrid Fix: Real-time inventory barcoding with automated inter-hospital transfer manifests.
REALITY 04 Solution: 100% Component Separation & Domestic Plasma Pooling

Overuse of Whole Blood vs. Component Separation

When one unit of whole blood is given to a patient who only needs red cells, the platelets and plasma in that unit are effectively lost. Non-component facilities experience 3× artificial scarcity, while facilities with component separators save 3 distinct lives with each single donation.

✓ HemaGrid Fix: Fractions tracked from phlebotomy chair; excess plasma sent to domestic factories for Albumin synthesis.
REALITY 05 Solution: 48-Hour Expiry Radar + Monthly Rolling Quotas

High Wastage and Short Shelf-Life

Platelets expire in just 5 days. Massive collection camps held on national holidays collect thousands of units that overwhelm storage and expire 35 days later, followed by 10 months of dry shelves and emergency shortages.

✓ HemaGrid Fix: Expiry Radar automatically transfers expiring platelets within 48h to active cancer/dengue wards.
REALITY 06 Solution: Anemia Nutrition Coaching + Rare Phenotype Registry

The Eligible Donor Paradox

Despite a large, young population, fear of pain, nutritional iron deficiency in willing women (Hb < 12.5), and extreme scarcity of Rh-negative (< 5%) and rare Bombay phenotypes leave emergency supplies vulnerable.

✓ HemaGrid Fix: Nurtures deferred female donors with 30-day nutrition coaching; geofences rare Bombay donors securely on WhatsApp.
